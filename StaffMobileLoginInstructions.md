# 📢 Announcement: You Can Now View Your Payslip Online!

Good news! You can now check your own payslip anytime through the RS Pet Stop Portal - no need to ask HR or wait for a printed copy.

## 🔗 Where to go

👉 **https://rspetstop.com/staff-login.html**

## 📱 How to install it on your phone (optional, but recommended)

### iPhone (Safari)
1. Open the link above in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** icon (square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen."**
4. Tap **Add.** You'll now have an "RS Pet Stop" icon on your home screen like a regular app.

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **3-dot menu** (top right).
3. Tap **"Add to Home screen"** or **"Install app."**
4. Tap **Add/Install.** The icon will appear on your home screen.

## 🔑 How to log in

Use the same username and password you already use for the portal.

## 📄 How to view your payslip

1. Log in.
2. Tap **"My Payslips"** in the menu.
3. You'll see your pay periods with Base Pay, Additions, Deductions, and Net Pay.
4. Tap **"View / Print"** on any period for the full breakdown.

## Good to know

- Only **your own** payslip shows up - private to you.
- It only appears once payroll for that period is **finalized**.
- Questions about your pay? Message the Payroll Officer as usual.
