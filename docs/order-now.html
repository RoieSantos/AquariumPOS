🛍️ RSPetstop Payment & Release Policy

We follow a 50/50 payment structure for all orders, including custom setups and stocked items.

💳 Payment Terms

  • 50% down payment is required to start processing.
  • Remaining 50% must be paid before or upon item release.
  • No item will be released without full payment.
👉 Payments are non-refundable.
👉 An Order Confirmation Receipt will be sent to you with your ORDER ID that will serve as your receipt. Please take a moment to read and confirm 🙂

📦 Order Processing

  • Work begins once the down payment is received.
  • Turnaround times vary per order and will be communicated.

⏱ Estimated Production Timeframes

  • Standard / Customized sizes (3mm–6mm): 2–5 days
  • Thicker glass (10mm or higher): 10–15 days
  • Low-iron or tempered glass: 15 working days preparation + 5 days fabrication
