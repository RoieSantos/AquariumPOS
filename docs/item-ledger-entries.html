Item Ledger Entries

Every stock movement, one signed line each - receipts add, issues subtract, and on-hand stock is simply the sum. Entries are permanent: a mistake is corrected by reversing it (which posts an opposite entry), never by editing or deleting, so the history always reconciles.

Movements that did not reach the ledger

These stock movements happened (a receipt or a transfer was saved) but could not be written to the ledger, so the balances below are missing them. Fix the cause (e.g. an item or variant that no longer exists), post the missing quantity as an adjustment above, then dismiss the row.
