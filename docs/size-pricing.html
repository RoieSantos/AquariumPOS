Size Pricing

For products whose price depends on size, like fish. A size scale adds a % and/or a fixed ₱ amount to the product's normal price for each size, then rounds it, e.g. SHBRTG ₱8,500 → 10-11" (+18%, round to 100) = ₱10,000. When staff sell a product on a scale, the POS, Conversations and Online Orders ask for the size and fill in that price. Stock isn't split by size. Order Now isn't affected. Alice tells customers fish are priced by size. Super users only.
