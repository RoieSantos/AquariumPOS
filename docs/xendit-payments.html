Xendit Payments

Online payments customers make on the Pay Online page (pay.html) through Xendit. A paid invoice is added to its order's payments automatically as "Xendit <channel>" (e.g. Xendit GCASH). TEST rows are test-mode payments (no real money), recorded as "Xendit TEST ..." - delete them from the order card after testing. Only visible to super users.
