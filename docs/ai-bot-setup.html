Follow-Ups

Teach the bot to proactively check back in on Messenger instead of only ever replying when a customer messages first. Each follow-up is composed fresh by the AI (using the directions below) rather than sent as fixed template text. Facebook only allows this within 24 hours of the customer's last message to the page - anything older is skipped automatically.

If the bot replied and the customer never answered back, send one friendly check-in after this many minutes.
After the bot hands a conversation to staff (complaints, refunds, etc.), have it check whether the customer already heard back.
If an order placed through the bot is still sitting as "New" (not yet confirmed/paid), remind the customer and give them the order number.
When the bot tells a customer mid-conversation "I'll check back with you tomorrow", it can queue that follow-up itself. Turn off to stop the bot from ever promising a callback.
