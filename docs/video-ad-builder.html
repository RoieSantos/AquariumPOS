Tips

  • Keep it about 15 seconds and the headlines short. Most people only read the first scene.
  • Facebook and Instagram cover the very top and the bottom third with buttons and captions, so text is always placed in the middle.
  • Vertical photos and clips (taken with the phone held upright) fill the screen best. Clips play muted.
  • The built-in music is made by the portal, so it's safe to post. If you use your own song, or add one from the Reels music library instead, Facebook may mute a song you don't have the rights to.
