📣 New Arrivals

When a Transfer Order with fish or plants is received at GMA or Amaya, a "NEW ARRIVALS" post goes up on that store's Facebook Page automatically (GMA → GMA Page, Amaya → RS Pet Stop Page). The photo is the banner below. The caption lists the item names, with no prices. The same arrivals also show on rspetstop.com and Alice knows about them.
